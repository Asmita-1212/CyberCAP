🔐 Cyber Safety Tips

  • Use strong, unique passwords for different accounts.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, or bank info with anyone.
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious links or email attachments.
  • Keep software and antivirus updated regularly.
  • Report cybercrimes at cybercrime.gov.in.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is phishing?

Phishing is a fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by disguising as a trustworthy entity via email or message.

How do I report a cybercrime?

You can report cybercrimes online at cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930.

What is ransomware?

Ransomware is malicious software that locks your files or system until a ransom is paid. Avoid downloading unknown files and back up your data regularly.

How can I stay safe on social media?

Keep accounts private, don’t overshare personal details, and report any abusive or fake profiles immediately.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?

Do not click on any links or attachments. Verify the sender’s address and report the email as phishing to your email provider.
